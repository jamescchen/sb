Pros & Cons Breakdown

Key tradeoffs to evaluate before deciding

Texas & New Mexico

Advantages (Pros)

  • Efficient one-way routing (AUS in, ELP out) eliminates ~600 miles of stressful backtrack driving.
  • Unmatched natural contrast: from Austin's music & food scene to Big Bend's canyons and White Sands' snow-white gypsum dunes.
  • Optimal winter weather with crisp, sunny daytime hiking temperatures in West Texas desert parks.
  • Authentic culinary experience with world-famous Texas brisket, Tex-Mex, and craft brews in Austin and Marfa.
  • World-class stargazing at Big Bend National Park, an official International Dark Sky Reserve with exceptionally clear December night skies.

Drawbacks (Cons)

  • Still involves substantial highway driving (~850 miles total) and one-way car rental drop-off fee.
  • West Texas desert nights get quite cold (frequently dropping near freezing in Dec).
  • Remote stretches in Big Bend have patchy cell service and long distances between gas stations.
  • Popular national park accommodations (Chisos Basin, Terlingua) require advance booking.
  • Extreme desert diurnal temperature swings require packing heavy multi-layer cold weather gear alongside light daytime clothing.

Sicily, Italy

Advantages (Pros)

  • No car rental necessary; comfortable express buses connect Palermo and Catania directly across the island.
  • Rich European cultural experience with millennial history, Greek theaters, and UNESCO cathedral mosaics.
  • Unforgettable Sicilian gastronomy: Arancini, fresh seafood, Mount Etna volcanic wines, and cannoli.
  • Low winter tourist crowds and festive Italian Christmas market atmosphere in Palermo & Catania.
  • Substantially lower off-season rates for boutique hotels, guided excursions, and dining compared to summer peak.

Drawbacks (Cons)

  • Longer transatlantic flight time and jet lag impact on a 9-day break.
  • Shorter winter daylight hours and chance of rain/cooler Mediterranean winds.
  • Bus schedules dictate arrival times, requiring sticking to set intercity timetables.
  • Some coastal beach venues and tourist shops operate on reduced off-season winter hours.
  • Higher elevation activities like Mount Etna cable cars can be subject to weather closures or snow/wind shutdowns in December.
